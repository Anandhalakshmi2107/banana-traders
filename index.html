Our Banana Varieties

9 traditional Tamil Nadu banana varieties — fresh, farm-sourced, and full of nutrition.

🍌 Fresh
பூவன் வாழை

Poovan

One of the most popular varieties in Tamil Nadu. Mildly sweet with a slightly tangy taste. Excellent for daily consumption and ideal for infants and elders.

Rich in Iron Daily Use All Ages
🍌 Fresh
செவ்வாழை

Sevvazhai

The red banana variety known for its deep reddish-purple peel. High in beta-carotene and antioxidants. Creamy, sweet flesh with a unique flavour profile.

Antioxidant Beta-Carotene Immunity
🍌 Fresh
ஏலக்கி வாழை

Elaki

A small and fragrant variety with a distinct cardamom-like aroma. Highly sought after for its pleasant sweetness and aromatic taste. Great for desserts.

Aromatic Sweet Desserts
🍌 Fresh
நேந்திரம் வாழை

Nendhiram

A firm, large banana ideal for cooking. Used in traditional Kerala and Tamil dishes like chips, halwa, and aviyal. Rich in potassium and dietary fibre.

Cooking Banana High Potassium Traditional
🍌 Fresh
நாட்டுக்காய் வாழை

Nattukai

A native country variety prized for its medicinal properties. Often used in traditional rituals and Ayurvedic practices. Dense, starchy, and nutritious.

Medicinal Native Variety Ayurvedic
🍌 Fresh
ரஸ்தாளி வாழை

Rasthali

A premium thin-skinned variety with a delicious custard-like taste. Known as the "Queen of Bananas". Very popular for offerings in temples and gifting.

Premium Thin-Skinned Temple Offering
🍌 Fresh
தேன்வாழை

Thenvaazhai

Meaning "Honey Banana" — this variety is naturally sweet as honey. Soft texture, golden yellow peel, and a rich flavour that kids and elders absolutely love.

Honey-Sweet Kids Favourite Soft Texture
🍌 Fresh
சக்கை வாழை

Sakkai

A robust and fibrous variety traditionally grown in rural Tamil Nadu. High in fibre and great for digestive health. Often dried or processed for snacks.

High Fibre Digestive Health Rural Variety
🍌 Fresh
காய்வாழை

Kaaivazhai

A green cooking banana variety that remains firm even when ripe. Ideal for curries, stir-fries, and traditional Tamil kootu dishes. A kitchen staple.

Cooking Use Firm Flesh Kitchen Staple
